Voxel Art Portfolio

I had the opportunity to work on multiple different game projects and on other freelance work as part of my work as a Voxel Artist. Here you can find a showcase of different projects I've created voxel art for including those within the games industry as well as other contractors. All this work was done using MagicaVoxel.

A portfolio of my best personal works can be found on Cara.

Teardown

2019 - 2020

Teardown is a very successfull game mainly consisting of voxel-based destruction created by TuxedoLabs.

I was part of the team working on it mainly during the development phase before its release into Early Access. During that time I mostly worked on assets for various vehicles as well as lots of different foliage. The image carousel shows an excerpt of my work on the game.

Part of my work on Teardown was also the creation of a fully fledged out castle scene. Originally meant as a showcase for Teardown's modding capacities it has since become the most used test scene by various different voxel rendering projects.

Links:
  • Teardown - Website / Teardown - Steam

Dawn Apart

2019 - 2023

Dawn Apart is a space colony and factory sim with fully destructible environments by Industrial Technology and Witchcraft.

I was on an off the project over the years working on assets in various stages of the game's development. Initially I worked on concept dioramas based on the client's requirements. At a later stage I then was commisioned to work on a larger assortment of different assets that can be seen in the game. Eventually I was also tasked with creating the landscape concept of one of the areas in the game. The image carousel shows an excerpt of my work.

Links:
  • Dawn Apart - Steam

Cloudpunk

2020

Cloudpunk is an open world neon-noir story game set in a cyberpunk metropolis by Ion Lands.

My task on the game was to create one of the skyscrapers that can be found in an area of the city.

Links:
  • Cloudpunk - Steam

Avoyd

2021

Avoyd is a voxel editor and renderer developed by enkisoftware.

My task was to create one of the main example scenes that is used to showcase the renderer's capabilites. Additionally enkisoftware gained the rights to use some of my personal projects for their rendering examples too.

Links:
  • Avoyd - Website

Rooms.xyz

2021 - 2023

Rooms.xyz is a collection of user-generated 3D spaces including mini games and interactive spaces by Things Inc.

As part of my work I had the opportunity to work on multiple different example rooms as well as helping with testing out different layouts for the rooms on the websie.

Links:
  • Rooms.xyz - Website

Voxile

2023 - 2026

Voxile is a game where you can quest, craft, loot, build and destroy on different voxel islands that are either prebuilt or procedurally generated developed by VoxRay Games, Inc.

I was one of the main voxel artists creating various block sets and unique assets throughout the game's development.
